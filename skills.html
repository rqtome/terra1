Abilities / Skills

A comprehensive list of Shidou Ryusei's skills and named abilities

Physicality: Shidou is one of the strongest and most physically dominant in the Blue lock facility, his grip strength alone was able to life Gurimu Igarashi (61KG) and kick back Rensuke Kunigami, one of the most physically capable in Blue Lock. He is able to use this in a variety of ways within matches.

Inverse Spacial Awareness: Shidou's most notable skill is his extreme spacial awareness. However, it's not the type of spacial awareness which takes into account the other players and their abilities on the field. It is more of a goal-centered awareness which makes him aware of where the goal is at all times, even when he might not necessarily be facing it. This makes him able to score from just about anywhere in the penalty area without even glancing at the goal. So it is less of a spacial areness which branches out and is aware of a multitude of variables, but is rather the type of awareness which is locked precisely on one variable at all times, that one variable being the goal.

Reflexes: Shidou can reflexively react to an oncoming goal and shoot it into the goal, his 'inverse' spacial awareness contributes to this and helps him reflexively shoot from anywhere in the penalty area.

Drive Shot: An accurate and powerful shot, and Shidou's main method of scoring. He is able to perform in from various positions, which is aided by his spacial awareness and reflexes.
